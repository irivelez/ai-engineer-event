Possible spoken line: "This year, research papers on agents, on long tasks and on training against checkable answers roughly quadrupled. Papers on models gaming their tests nearly tripled. AI papers overall grew by half." Label: estimate (our count). Caveats:
- This measures how often a topic is discussed, not citations; the MCP gives no citation counts.
- 2026 is not over yet.
- The index dates papers by their latest arXiv version.
- Three topics grew by nearly the same factor (×4.0–4.1); we can't explain that coincidence, so treat the factors as approximate.
- The control phrase rules out the index simply growing fourfold.

**2. Capability on paper versus output in practice.** Rows 1 and 2 together make one point the keynote can use in "motor". On the same real projects, models went from 2.5% to 41% in a year (Remote Labor Index). Yet developers given the stronger model didn't merge more work (Hua, 17 Nov). TAI #229 gives the business version: "A lender could buy a model that reviews a loan file in ten minutes and still take two years to change who has the authority to approve it."
