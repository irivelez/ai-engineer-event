# Fonts

Latin subsets downloaded from Google Fonts on 17 Nov 2026 and bundled so the keynote works offline.
These Google Fonts families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

| File | Family | Role in the keynote |
|---|---|---|
| `newsreader-var.woff2`, `newsreader-italic-var.woff2` | Newsreader | Quotes |
| `archivo-var.woff2` | Archivo | Names, act titles |
| `doto-var.woff2` | Doto | Dot-matrix numbers and timecodes |
| `plexmono-400.woff2`, `plexmono-500.woff2` | IBM Plex Mono | Labels |
